HEALTHCARE AI CASE STUDY

MediDx AI

AI-Assisted Health Assessment & Clinical Decision Support — a portfolio prototype designed around deterministic red-flag screening, structured synthetic patient intake, evidence-aware differential considerations, clinician-review workflows and explicit safety boundaries.

PythonFastAPISQLAlchemyPostgreSQLDockerRender
Open Live Demo ↗ GitHub Repository ↗

Problem

Clinical intake contains symptoms, history, vital signs and risk factors that must be organized quickly. A prototype in this space also needs strong boundaries so it does not present model output as a confirmed diagnosis or autonomous treatment decision.

Solution

MediDx AI structures synthetic patient information, runs deterministic red-flag checks first, classifies urgency, produces differential considerations with supporting and missing evidence, highlights information gaps and provides clinician-oriented next-step guidance.

Architecture

Clinical IntakeSymptoms, age, sex, duration, onset, history, allergies, medications, smoking status and vitals.
Deterministic Safety EngineRed-flag rules and urgency classification run ahead of any future generative-AI layer.
Decision-Support LayerDifferential considerations, evidence for/against, missing information and clinician next steps.
Persistence & AuditPatient/encounter IDs, assessment history and audit summaries using SQLAlchemy with PostgreSQL-ready persistence.

Engineering Highlights

  • FastAPI REST API with structured Pydantic request and response models.
  • Rule-based emergency screening for symptoms and abnormal vital-sign thresholds.
  • Evidence-aware differential cards rather than a single definitive diagnosis.
  • Assessment history with encounter reopen workflow.
  • Docker Compose development environment and Render cloud deployment.

Safety Boundary

Demo — not for clinical use. MediDx AI uses synthetic scenarios and does not diagnose disease, prescribe treatment or replace a licensed clinician. The current release intentionally uses deterministic logic rather than an unconstrained LLM for emergency screening.

Roadmap

  • Controlled AI summarization and follow-up-question generation after safety screening.
  • FHIR-compatible patient and observation structures.
  • RBAC and clinician authentication.
  • Lab and medication context.
  • MediVoice AI integration for voice-based clinical intake and retrieval.
